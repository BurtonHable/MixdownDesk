Start here

Signal flows from the stage to the speakers: source → mic or DI → cable → stage box input → channel preamp (GAIN) → HPF, COMP, EQ → FX send and PAN → fader → stereo master → main speakers. AUX sends feed the monitors. PFL feeds your headphones.

TryFollow the Next step box above. The board starts zeroed and unplugged, and the house stays silent until the main speakers are patched. Or pick “Ready to play” from the Setup menu to hear the band right away.

Your stems

Load one audio file per channel (WAV, MP3, M4A or AIFF). Files stay in your browser and are never uploaded. Long files are trimmed to the section you choose below. Stems were already mic’d when they were recorded, so the mic you choose on stage adds its tone on top.

Need stems? The Cambridge-MT multitrack library has hundreds of free multitracks for classroom use. Download a song, unzip it, and pick one track per source.

Stage

Drag performers, monitors and main speakers to move them. Round handles aim a speaker or the selected mic; the square handle moves that mic. Colored dots show status.
